Power Conversion Technologies

Active Harmonic Filters

Veeral's active harmonic filters monitor electrical loads in real time and inject counter-harmonic currents to keep power quality high. They remove harmonics, stabilize voltage, and improve plant efficiency.

  • Digital real-time control for precise harmonic cancellation
  • Power factor correction and voltage stabilization
  • Modular design that scales to higher ratings
  • Protection for sensitive industrial equipment

Applications: Facilities with heavy nonlinear loads, such as steel mills, solar plants, drive and converter systems, and research labs
